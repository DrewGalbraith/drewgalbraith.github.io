The power of the press
in sustaining
the work he had begun, was early recog-
nized

Smith

The Church had been organized

a conference

pendence,

In

first periodical issued by the Church.
The

this

mob, which collected at Independence to

Saints from Jackson County.
This press and the property was never
again

to

Church, though
the Star
was

published
in
Kirtland, edited by Oliver Cowdery.
Another

the
Elders'
1
Journal.
But

Joseph
Smith and Sidney Rigdon had to flee for
their

the

published were removed to Far West.
Here an effort was made to re-issue the
Journal, Sidney Rigdon being appointed

But

The form

This

Its

Smith,

at the time he took charge of the Times

of

His

Robinson.
The paper was
first

Don Carlos Smith continued to
act as
the editor of this paper
until

of Aug-
ust,

Ebenezer Robinson then be-
came

editor and
Elder Robert

The manner in which the paper was con-
ducted was

President

It was

resigned the

"And," says Joseph,

Need we stop to moralize on the

On

As soon as he had read one of them, he looked

"What can
I do?
I can do nothing for you!
If

I
shall come in contact with

I cannot determine whether
